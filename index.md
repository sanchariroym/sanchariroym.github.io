    <h1>Sanchari Roy</h1>

    <p>Sanchari Roy is Associate Professor of Economics and Director of the MRes/PhD Programme at the
    <a href="https://business-school.exeter.ac.uk/about/departments/economics/">Department of Economics</a>,
    <a href="https://business-school.exeter.ac.uk">University of Exeter</a>.</p>

    <p>Her research sits at the intersection of gender, development economics and psychology. She studies
    how laws, norms and beliefs shape women's lives: how inheritance reforms affect son preference and
    dowry, how stigma and self-image affect marginalised women, and how poverty and mental health
    interact for women and adolescents. Much of this work uses randomised trials and natural experiments
    in India, with recent projects in Vietnam, Colombia, Nepal and South Africa. Her work has appeared in
    the <em>Review of Economics and Statistics</em>, the <em>Economic Journal</em> and the
    <em>Journal of Development Economics</em>.</p>

    <p>Before Exeter, she taught at King's College London, the University of Warwick and the University of Sussex.
    She is an Invited Researcher at <a href="https://www.povertyactionlab.org/invited-researchers">J-PAL</a>,
    Research Associate at <a href="https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/economics/research/centres/cage/">CAGE, University of Warwick</a>,
    and Researcher at the <a href="https://www.theigc.org">International Growth Centre</a>.</p>
