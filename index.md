    <h1>Sanchari Roy</h1>
    <div class="bio">
    <p>Sanchari Roy is <strong class="role">Associate Professor of Economics</strong> and Director of the MRes/PhD Programme at the
    <a href="https://business-school.exeter.ac.uk/about/departments/economics/">Department of Economics</a>,
    <a href="https://business-school.exeter.ac.uk">University of Exeter</a>.
    She is an Invited Researcher at <a href="https://www.povertyactionlab.org/invited-researchers">J-PAL</a>,
    Research Associate at <a href="https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/economics/research/centres/cage/">CAGE, University of Warwick</a>,
    and Researcher at the <a href="https://www.theigc.org">International Growth Centre</a>.</p>

    <p>Sanchari's research sits at the intersection of gender, development economics and psychology.
    She studies how laws, norms and beliefs shape the lives of women and their children, using experimental
    and quasi-experimental methods in India, Vietnam, Colombia, Nepal and South Africa. Sanchari has also
    taught at King's College London, the University of Warwick and the University of Sussex.</p>
